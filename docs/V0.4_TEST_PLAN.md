## Manual gameplay
1. Start from an existing V0.3-complete save, enter Lumen Wilds and confirm the Basin Gate is available.
2. Enter Aether Basin.
3. Confirm the Archivist is reachable and starts the Anchor Array mission.
4. Activate all four anchors and verify one-time rewards/progression.
5. Confirm the mission advances to the Resonance Vault.
6. Activate the vault and verify the return lift unlocks.
7. Collect both Basin shards and confirm they stay collected after reload.
8. Set the Basin checkpoint, touch a hazard, and confirm safe respawn.
9. Use RADAR and NAV across the Basin.
10. Wait through at least one 18-second event cycle and confirm a Resonance Surge event fires.
11. Speak with the Runner after anchor activation, complete the Resonance Sprint, and verify the four-gate route, timer, rewards and best-time record.
12. Return to Lumen Wilds and confirm its mission state remains intact.
13. Reload from BASIN and verify state restoration.
